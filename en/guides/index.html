Why four ways, and not one best one

Because money does not arrive the same way for everybody. A steady paycheck and a shop till cannot use the same chart. The best method is the one you can keep up for months in a row, not the one that looks best the day you set it. So every guide also says who it is not for, and points at one of the others when it is not a fit.
